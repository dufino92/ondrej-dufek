03 / How I work

What I look for in a product.

  1. 01

    Start with the customer. Who is it for, and why would they buy it?

  2. 02

    Make something worth eating. Good ingredients, a recipe that makes sense and a clear purpose.

  3. 03

    Make sure we can produce it with the same quality every day, not just once at a tasting.

  4. 04

    See what people actually buy. Look at waste and results. If it doesn’t work, change it or stop selling it.

A great sandwich nobody buys is a bad product.
