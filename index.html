03 / How I work

How I work on a new product.

  1. 01

    Start with the customer. Where will they eat it, and why would they choose it?

  2. 02

    Give the recipe a reason to exist. Taste and ingredients matter, but it needs a purpose.

  3. 03

    Check that production can deliver the same quality tomorrow, not just at the tasting.

  4. 04

    Look at what sells and what gets wasted. If a product is not working, change it or take it off the shelf.

The kitchen is still where I start when a product needs fixing. The shelf tells me whether we got it right.
